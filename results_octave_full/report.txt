10. EVIDENCE LEVELS AND LIMITATIONS
- Exact: singular-value derivative formulas, delay-margin crossing condition, additivity of the complex modal truncation.
- Numerical (double precision, not interval arithmetic): eigenvalues, Newton-refined exact-delay roots, tail bounds, all self-checks.
- Empirical (finite grids): completeness of the valley lists, switch locations, screening statistics, timing (this machine only).
- Not covered: sampled-data implementation, actuator rate limits and friction, model uncertainty beyond the listed corners, flight data, physical attribution of valleys to modes (counterfactuals only).
